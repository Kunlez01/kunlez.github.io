E-commerce Market Analysis (Dashboard Audit)

Audited a management dashboard built on 432 retail transactions across five Nigerian states, three sales channels and three product categories. I cleaned the data in Power Query by removing duplicate records and standardising inconsistent state and category labels, then rebuilt the dashboard in Power BI with KPI cards, state, category, channel and monthly views, and a New/Returning customer filter.

The corrected view showed that Lagos, not Abuja, was the most profitable market, and that In-store had the lowest return rate (6.4%), ahead of Online (9.7%) and WhatsApp (11.9%). That corrected the claim that Online had the lowest return rate.

Independent project based on the DSN AI Bootcamp 2026 data analytics challenge brief.
